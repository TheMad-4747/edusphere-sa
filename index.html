About EduSphere SA

Learn. Understand. Succeed.

EduSphere SA is a free South African educational platform for learners following the DBE/CAPS curriculum in Grades 8–12. It is designed to be practical, fast and mobile-friendly — including on low-end smartphones and slow connections.

How it works

Every document is embedded inside this single page: a fast plain-text version for previewing online, and the original Word document encoded for download. Nothing is loaded from external folders or servers, so the platform works offline once the page is saved.

Content policy

Demonstration resources are EduSphere-original sample materials. They are clearly labelled and are not official DBE documents. Genuine DBE papers, when added, will be labelled as such.

Disclaimer

EduSphere SA is an independent platform and is not endorsed by the DBE, any provincial education department, school, circuit or district.
